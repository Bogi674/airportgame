## Root causes from the before shots

* GA scene (`handoff/work/T57-before-ga/`): the GA row sits about 16 m from a code C taxiway centerline, inside the wingtip envelope of passing traffic. Its apron stops short of the taxiway, so the lead in arcs run over grass, and the taxiway edge line becomes a chain of fillet arcs, one per stand.
* `makeStands` and `buildGaBlock` (`src/sim/airfield/airfield.ts`) check strip, apron and stand overlap only. No taxiway clearance check exists; `src/shared/clearance.ts` only warns.
* `src/sim/vehicles/gse.ts` drives every vehicle on a straight line from 40 m out to a hard coded offset that ignores vehicle width, wing and engines. `gse.json` has no docking data.
* Vehicles are drawn from the raw snapshot with no interpolation, so they lag a moving aircraft. Vehicle shadows are a flat offset rect.
* Draw order already puts vehicles under aircraft (`renderer.ts` frame draw), but people are drawn under vehicles and aircraft, against `docs/ART_AND_ASSETS.md` section 3.

## Design

* Clearance (user D1, refined): the stand box must clear every taxiway centerline it does not lead into by `taxiObjectM` of that taxiway's code. For the taxiway it leads into, the box must clear the centerline by half the code span (`spanM / 2` of that code) plus the stand size `wingtipClearanceM`, which is the wingtip envelope of the largest passing aircraft. Recorded in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 12.
* Shared function `standTaxiwayClash` in `src/shared/clearance.ts`, used by sim validation and the ghost.
* Old saves (D2): derived list of breaching stand ids on the airfield payload, recomputed on refresh, never saved.
* GA block apron extends to the lead in taxiway pavement so the fork runs on pavement and the edge line opens along the frontage.
* Footprint port of `gse.py` `boundary` and `check` into `src/shared/aircraftFootprint.ts`.
* Docking poses per vehicle kind in `src/data/gse.json` keyed by service point, waypoint approach in the aircraft frame (D3 exceptions: refueller and hydrant dispenser under the wing when the wing underside is above the vehicle height).
* Save format change: none.
