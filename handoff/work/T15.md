## Design

Decisions settled with the user on 2026-11-11:

* Recipe weights per profile are drafted in `src/data/people.json`, one integer per profile per recipe, tunable later.
* The atlas is baked lazily, the first time the zoom enters a tier band, and then stays cached.
* Each tier bakes at the top of its band: 8 px/m for the dot tier, 24 px/m for torso and head, 64 px/m for the full figure.
* The cap counts figures. The renderer keeps the sample entries nearest the camera center first. Density scaling waits for T16.

Decisions taken by the orchestrator:

* Snapshot version 5 adds `peopleSeed` and `peopleHeading` with no save change.
* Queued cohorts stand in queue cells by rank, one group per 2 m cell with occupancy marks, facing the BFS parent cell.
* Members are packed by footprint with a 0.05 m margin, and members that do not fit are not drawn.
* Walking and airside cohorts are not sampled until the route following task.
* The cap counts figures nearest the camera center, 3 000 or 1 500 under the governor.
* The crowd check runs at 4 px/m because people are hidden below 4 px/m.
* Density scaling waits for T16.
* A recipe fixes skin tone, hair and clothes, so the atlas is 30 recipes by 4 frames per tier in 1.6 m cells.
* The baker is split in two: pure shape ops ported from `people2.py`, and a rasterizer. Tests run on the ops.
* The old dot sample in `src/render/people.ts` is replaced, not kept.

## Slices

1. Snapshot seed: `peopleSeed` and `peopleHeading`, version 5, written by `writeSample`.
2. Data: `people.json`, schema, `PEOPLE` export, weighted recipe pick.
3. Shape ops: body, head, hair, tiers and walk frames.
4. Shape ops: items, overlays, wheelchair, bounds test for every recipe.
5. Atlas and renderer: lazy tier atlas, nearest first selection, renderer wiring, old dot sample removed.
5b. Queue cell placement and footprint packing.
6. Scenario, screenshots, frame time, docs and roadmap.
