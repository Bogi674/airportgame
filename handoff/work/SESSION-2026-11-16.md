## Done and committed (01c8286, quick verify green)

Player report: landed GA aircraft never took off again on a one way loop taxiway joined to both runway ends, and later arrivals were refused with "no free stand" while stands looked empty.

Root causes, both in the taxi network:

1. `deriveNetwork` (`src/sim/airfield/geometry.ts`) made one runway entry per taxiway and runway pair. A single taxiway that meets the runway at both ends got one entry, so the downstream end had no holding point.
2. `TaxiNetwork.assemble` (`src/sim/airfield/network.ts`) dropped the last stretch of a line whose final vertices merged into one node, for example a taxiway ending exactly on a runway threshold.

Parked aircraft therefore found no route to a holding point, stayed in turnaround forever and kept their stands. Fixes: one entry per touching stretch, the nearest holding point per entry, the trailing chain is flushed. In `src/sim/ops/traffic.ts` arrivals now try the next best runway end when no exit of the best end reaches any usable stand (`exitReachesStand`), the refusal says `noRoute` in that case, and a departure that cannot leave reports `crosswind` or `noRouteOut` once. Regression test: `tests/loopTaxiway.test.ts`.
