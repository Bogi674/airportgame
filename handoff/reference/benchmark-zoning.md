# Benchmark: zoning and transitions

Summary of public wiki and forum material on the zoning mechanic of the benchmark game, gathered 2026-11-24 for T88. Facts only, in this project's words. Sources: the benchmark wiki pages Zones, Security, International Zone and Overlays (airportceo.wiki.gg), and two forum threads on secure, staff and international zoning (forum.apoglabs.com, threads 4541 and 18320).

* Zones are painted over floor on top of an implicit public area: Secure, Staff and International. International is painted on top of an area that is already secure.
* Passengers and staff move from non secure to secure only through a security checkpoint, and leave secure only through a security exit. A security exit may be zoned staff only.
* Checkpoints come in sizes with more officers and more speed. Passport control (one officer) and an automated passport gate (no officer, needs research) lead into International.
* Shops, food, toilets and seating belong in the secure area. Departures leave from secure.
* Common player failures: gaps in the secure paint (stairs and escalators) cut paths; two secure areas with no link make passengers pick a checkpoint with no path; removing a checkpoint leaves its zone painted.
* Not documented in the sources found: whether placing a checkpoint paints any zone, the zone colors, or the warning icons. This game's own rules in `docs/TERMINAL_ZONING.md` and T88 fill those gaps.
