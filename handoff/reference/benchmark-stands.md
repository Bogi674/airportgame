# Benchmark notes: stands in Airport CEO

Our own summary of the stand mechanics, from the community wiki page the user supplied on 2026-11-12. Mechanics only. No text, art or screenshots of that game go into this repository. Revision 4 section 2 and the T59 and T66 specs use these notes when a stand rule is unclear.

## Sizes

* Three sizes: small (general aviation and small commercial), medium, large. This game keeps its five real scale classes (GA, small, medium, large, extra large).

## Per stand settings

* Flight types the stand accepts: GA, commercial, or commercial for one airline only. Large stands can be limited to medium flights.
* International or domestic only.
* Night flight access on or off. Booked flights stay when it is switched off; emergency flights ignore it.
* Included in the auto planner or not.
* Open or closed. Closing is only allowed when no aircraft is tied to the stand, including aircraft queued at a runway for it, and it drops all booked flights after a confirmation prompt.
* Demolish only from the stand's own panel, and only when closed.
* Force an aircraft at the stand to leave.

## Connections and assignments

* Linked to a baggage bay; the link can only change while no aircraft is tied to the stand.
* Belongs to a terminal; boarding desks are assigned to it, several desks per stand allowed. International stands need desks in the international zone.
* Service vehicle stop and airside shuttle bus stops (normal and arrival only) are assigned to the stand.
* Dedicated vehicles can be assigned per service: boarding, baggage, push back.
* Settings that touch an active turnaround are locked while an aircraft is tied to the stand.

## Servicing

* Any requested service needs a service road connection from the stand to a depot or vehicle parking, and ramp staff need a path from a security zone.
* Small stands are reached by fuel and emergency vehicles, plus baggage for commercial flights. Medium and large stands add the full commercial service set.
* Ramp staff per turnaround: small 2, medium 4, large 6. Medium uses one vehicle per service; large uses two catering trucks and one vehicle for each other service.

## Upgrades

* Surface: grass, asphalt or concrete for small; asphalt or concrete for medium and large.
* Jet bridge build or remove on medium and large.
* Repair, automatic when an operations manager is hired.

## Remote stands

* Any stand away from the terminal. Passengers ride an airside bus from a bus stop at a terminal exit with a boarding desk; the bus parks at the stand like other service vehicles.
* Ramp staff reach it from a service car stop at the terminal.
* The planner adds 45 minutes to a flight at a remote stand for travel time.
