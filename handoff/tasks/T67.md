# T67. Individuals inside cohorts: traits, needs, sub cohorts, satisfaction

Phase 3. Spec written 2026-11-18 on branch `track/t54`, right after T54 closed. It replaces T17 (satisfaction) and T18 (cohort stress). T20 (arrivals) and T68 (staff) start from it, and T32 (tenants) counts sales from its visits.

## Goal

The people inside a cohort are individuals. Each member has traits regenerated from a seed, needs that grow linearly in time, a budget and wants, and a satisfaction of its own. At decision points of the journey a member whose need passes its threshold leaves the group as a sub cohort, visits a fixture that serves the need, and rejoins the group or meets it at the gate. Member satisfaction rolls up to the cohort and, over a trailing 30 day window, to the airport rating. A medium stress scenario holds 20 000 cohorts and at least 40 000 people under 4 ms per tick.

Phase exit part owned here: a passenger who gets hungry leaves the group, visits a shop and rejoins at the gate, and the rating reacts.

## Facts found in the code

* A cohort has `size` (Uint8), `profile`, `spendBudget` and `satisfaction` (Uint8, set to 100 at generation and never changed). `satisfaction` and `flags` are saved but not hashed.
* Group size is `rng.int(1, PROFILES.maxGroup)` with `maxGroup` 6, one rng draw per cohort. D24 asks for sizes per profile.
* Airside cohorts wake every `RETRY_TICKS` (100) in `toGate` until their flight is on the ground, then walk to a gate desk. This is the only repeating decision point after security.
* Service nodes exist for check in, kiosk, bag drop, security, passport, gate desk and toilet (`SERVICE_KEYS`). Shops and seating are not service nodes. Toilets have 6 servers and 90 s in `business.json` but no cohort uses them.
* The airport rating's passenger part is the mean of toilets, shops, `queues`, on time, cleanliness and bags. `queues` is `Business.satisfaction`, an EMA of boarded over booked passengers updated in `onPushback`.
* Stage values are saved. Every stage range check (`st < Stage.Curb || st > Stage.Gate`) assumes stages 1 to 8 are the live ones.
* Save version is 27. `serviceLoad` refuses a table whose node count changed, so adding a node kind to `SERVICE_KEYS` would drop queues of old saves.
* The stress baseline is only written when the file is missing or with `--update-baseline`; a scenario absent from the baseline is budget checked but not regression checked.
* 1 tick is 0.1 s of motion, a game minute is 50 ticks, a game hour 3 000 ticks. Walking is motion time, visits and needs are calendar time.

## Decisions

1. **Members in fixed blocks.** Each cohort slot owns a block of `M = PROFILES.maxGroup` member slots; member `i` of cohort `c` is index `c * M + i`, and members `0 .. size[c] - 1` are live. Per member typed arrays: `memberSeed` (Uint32), `memberNeed` (Uint16, `NEED_COUNT` per member), `memberSat` (Uint8, 0 to 100). About 18 MB at the 200 000 cohort capacity, allocated once. (Rev 4 section 6.1, hard rule 5.)
2. **Traits are regenerated, state is saved** (section 6.2). `memberSeed` is identity and is saved; every trait is a pure integer hash of it (`src/sim/cohorts/traits.ts`), never drawn from an rng stream, so traits never shift a stream. Saved member state is only `memberSeed`, the need levels and `memberSat`.
3. **Traits in T67** (section 6.2, D25, D26): four need factors 0.7 to 1.3 (D25), shopping desire 0 to 1 and budget `profile.spend` times 0.5 to 1.5 (D26), two favourite shop categories (D26), how early the member likes to reach the gate, and mobility. Travel class follows the profile (premium is the premium class) and price sensitivity has nothing to act on before prices exist; both are added by T32 with prices, so T67 ships no unused trait.
4. **Needs** (section 6.3, D25): hunger, thirst, toilet and rest grow linearly in calendar time from the curb; each reaches its threshold after `thresholdH` (3, 2, 2.5, 0.667 game hours) divided by the member's factor. A level is stored in units where 1 000 is the threshold, brought up to date only at decision points from a per cohort `needTick`. A fifth slot, `shop`, holds the shopping want: desire times 1 000 at generation, it does not grow, and a member wants to shop when it is at or above `shopThreshold` times 1 000.
5. **Decision points** are the airside wakes of a cohort (stage `Airside`, after security and on every retry), the retail window of `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` section 8. Landside time still grows needs, which are first acted on airside. Never every tick.
6. **Which member goes where.** At a decision point the most urgent want is the largest level over its threshold (ties: lowest need index, then lowest member index). The members of the cohort with the same need over its threshold go together. The target is the cheapest reachable airside point of that need's fixtures by walk time, as `pickNode` does: hunger to quickFood or cafe, thirst to cafe or convenience, toilet to a toilet service node, rest to seating, shop to the member's two favourite categories. A visit starts only if `now + walk there + visit + walk back <= departure - gate lead` of the leading member; otherwise nothing changes this time.
7. **Sub cohorts.** When some but not all members go, they split into a new cohort slot with flag `SUB`, `parent` set to the main cohort (a sub of a sub points at the main cohort) and its members moved, in order; the parent closes the gap keeping member order. When every member goes, the cohort itself visits and keeps its slot. A full store means no split this time.
8. **Visits.** Toilets use the existing toilet service queue (servers and service time already in data). Food, drink, shop and rest are timed visits with no queue, `visitMin` game minutes at the fixture, because shop capacity belongs to T32. After the visit the need level of the visiting members resets to 0, and the visit is counted per need kind with its spend (`spendShare` of each member's budget) in the day counters.
9. **Rejoin or meet at the gate.** After the visit a sub cohort walks back to its parent while the parent is still `Airside`, of the same flight and not a sub, and merges into it on arrival (members appended, slot freed). Otherwise it becomes an airside cohort of its own and walks to the gate when its flight is on the ground, like any cohort. A sub cohort still out at pushback misses the flight.
10. **New stages are appended**: `ToVisit` 11, `Visit` 12, `VisitQueue` 13, `Rejoin` 14. Every stage list in `cohorts.ts` (pushback, grid removal, eviction, walker reset, done drain, sample passes) is updated: `ToVisit` and `Rejoin` are walking stages, `Visit` a wheel stage, `VisitQueue` a queued stage.
11. **Visit points** are derived per grid, never saved: the anchors of seating and the six shop fixtures with their walk node and side. A point is airside when its cell is secure, or commercial with the commercial area touching secure cells and no public ones (`docs/TERMINAL_ZONING.md` section 1); landside the reverse; otherwise unusable. Built when `serviceVersion` changes and on load. A sub cohort stores its target as the anchor array cell and finds the point again by cell and kind after an edit (PITFALLS 26); a lost point ends the visit with no reset.
12. **Walk speed per cohort.** A cohort with a reduced mobility member walks at `slowFactor` times its profile speed (flag `SLOW`, recomputed whenever members change), in `walk`, `pickNode` and the people sample alike.
13. **Group sizes per profile** (D24) replace `rng.int(1, maxGroup)` with a weighted pick from `groupSize` weights in `profiles.json`, still one rng draw per cohort. `maxGroup` stays 6 and is the block size.
14. **Member satisfaction** follows the formula of `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` section 8 (D27), in whole points from 100, clamped 0 to 100:
    * queue wait: every queue (check in, security, gate desk, toilet) costs `queuePerMin` per game minute waited over the profile's `patienceS`, to every member of the cohort;
    * walking: each walk leg costs `walkPerM` per metre over `walkComfortM`;
    * unmet need: at boarding or a miss, each need still over its threshold costs `unmetPerHour` per game hour it has been over;
    * missed flight: `missedPenalty` (40).
    The delay term is left out because punctuality is already its own rating part (owner, 2026-11-17), and the comfort, cleanliness, variety and wayfinding bonuses stay as the rating's toilets, shops and cleanliness parts. The cohort `satisfaction` is the mean of its members.
15. **Roll up to the rating** (D27, the T17 rule "keeps its existing parts and replaces its satisfaction input"): at boarding or a miss every member's satisfaction is added to the day counters. At the daily close the day goes into a 30 day ring; `Business.satisfaction` becomes the ring's member weighted mean divided by 100, and stays as it is while the ring holds no member. The served share EMA in `Business.onPushback` is removed. The rating formula is otherwise unchanged.
16. **Save version 28.** Cohorts gain `needTick`, `parent`, `waitStart`, `target` (Int32) and `visitNeed` (Uint8) per slot, the member arrays packed up to `hi * M`, the day counters and the 30 day ring. Migration 27 to 28 seeds members of saved cohorts from their `recipeSeed` and index, sets needs 0, `memberSat` 100, `needTick` to the save tick, `parent` -1, `waitStart` -1 (no wait penalty), `target` -1 and `visitNeed` 0.
17. **Hash order**, per slot after the T54 folds: `flags`, `needTick`, `parent`, `waitStart`, `target`, `visitNeed`, `satisfaction`, then per live member `memberSeed`, the five need levels, `memberSat`; after the slot loop the day counters, the ring head and the ring.
18. **Stress.** A new scenario `people20k` with its own terminal (check in, security, gate desks, seating, toilets and every shop kind airside) reaches at least 20 000 live cohorts and 40 000 active people at its peak, under 4 ms mean. It is not added to `perf-baseline.json` (PITFALLS 21); the other scenarios must not regress over 10 percent.
19. **Hooks for the next tasks.** `initMembers(c)` and `rollUp(c, missed)` are the only member entry and exit points, so T20 arrival cohorts reuse them; the queue wait term is generic for any queued stage, so T20's immigration, claim and customs waits count with no new code; visit points carry a side so landside visits can be added; `recordVisit` is the single place a visit is counted, where T32 adds per unit sales; T68 staff can reuse the member block and traits module with the crew profile.
20. **No render change.** Sub cohorts are cohorts, so the T54 sample draws them walking to and dwelling at their point; the close checks it on a screenshot.

## Proposed values (owner to review, per the 2026-11-18 phase 3 run)

| Key | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `profiles.json` `groupSize.family` | sizes 3, 4, 5 at 34, 33, 33 | D24 gives 3 to 5 without a split |
| `profiles.json` `groupSize.budget` and `.premium` | 1 at 60, 2 at 40 | D24 gives 1 or 2 without a split |
| `profiles.json` `groupSize.crew` | 1 at 100 | Crew travels alone |
| `needs.json` `shopThreshold` | 0.5 | Half the members want to shop with desire uniform 0 to 1 |
| `needs.json` `visit.hunger` | 20 min, spend share 0.4 | A quick meal |
| `needs.json` `visit.thirst` | 10 min, spend share 0.15 | A drink to go |
| `needs.json` `visit.shop` | 15 min, spend share 0.6 | Browse and buy, most of the budget |
| `needs.json` `visit.rest` | 20 min, spend share 0 | One sit down |
| `needs.json` `visit.toilet` | spend share 0, queue from `business.json` | Existing toilet service data |
| `needs.json` `gateLeadMin` | 15 to 40 game minutes | Most passengers want to be at the gate 15 to 40 minutes early |
| `needs.json` `mobility` | share 0.03, slowFactor 0.7 | A few slower walkers per flight |
| `needs.json` `satisfaction.queuePerMin` | 1 point per game minute over patience | Ten minutes over patience is a clear complaint |
| `needs.json` `satisfaction.walkComfortM`, `walkPerM` | 300 m, 0.02 per m | Two points per 100 m over a comfortable leg |
| `needs.json` `satisfaction.unmetPerHour` | 10 points per game hour over threshold | Below a missed flight, above a short queue |

Documented values used as written: D24 business, leisure; D25 thresholds and factor 0.7 to 1.3; D26 budget factor 0.5 to 1.5, desire 0 to 1, two favourites; D27 30 day window and missed flight 40.

## Acceptance criteria

* A member made hungry in a group of two or more airside splits off, walks to a quickFood fixture, dwells, has hunger below threshold, and either merges back into its group (size restored) or boards with the flight. The visit is counted.
* The same run without the food fixture ends with a lower member satisfaction for that passenger and, after the daily close, a lower `Business.satisfaction` and passenger score than the run with it.
* 2 000 ticks twice from one seed with splits give equal `stateHash`; a save mid visit restores to the same hash after 2 000 more ticks.
* A version 27 save loads with seeded members, full satisfaction and no visits in progress.
* `npm run stress`: `people20k` peak at least 20 000 live cohorts and 40 000 people, mean under 4 ms; medium under 4 ms; no regression over 10 percent; baseline untouched.
* Full `npm run verify` and the e2e smoke pass. The finance sheet shows the new row in the dev build.
