# T54. People walk and dwell along routes with spacing

Phase 3. Spec written 2026-11-18. Runs beside T75 in a second session; it touches the cohort store, the people sample and the people renderer only.

## Goal

Walking cohorts are drawn moving along their real walking graph route, from the walk node they left to the walk node they head for, at their profile walk speed, interpolated between snapshots. Dwelling cohorts (airside, waiting at a target, at a gate) are drawn standing at their place. Members of a group never stack, and groups walking the same stretch keep a gap. The sim stores only what it needs to derive positions, never pixels, and the journey timing does not change.

## Facts found in the code

* A cohort stores its target service node (`node`) and a wake tick only. `walk()` sets the wake tick from `walkDistance` and forgets where the walk started.
* `writeSample` draws only queued cohorts, one per 2 m queue cell.
* Walk node ids are renumbered on every walk graph rebuild (PITFALLS 25) and service node ids on every service rebuild (PITFALLS 26). Logical cells are stable across both.
* The walk graph is undirected, so the cached Dijkstra row of a target holds the distance from every node to that target.
* The walk cache is least recently used with 64 rows per grid. Results never depend on what is cached, but rows filled for the sample could evict rows the sim uses.
* Save version is 26. Cohort slot arrays are packed up to `hi`; a key missing from a save leaves the array untouched.
* Snapshots go out at 20 Hz; at speed 1 that is about one per tick, so the sample must stay cheap.

## Decisions

1. **Route state in the sim.** Each cohort slot gains `walkFromX`, `walkFromY`, `walkToX`, `walkToY` (Int16, logical cells of the walk nodes left and headed for) and `walkStart` (Int32, the tick the walk began, -1 for no walk). Cells, not node ids, so an edit never makes them stale. The route itself is derived at sample time and never saved.
2. **Journey timing is unchanged.** Wake ticks, node choice and the rng stream stay exactly as today. T54 adds state and drawing only. A walker whose target node is lost by an edit keeps its walk cells, is drawn going on to the old target cell, and replans from the curb when due as today.
3. **Position along the route without search.** At sample time the walked distance is `max(0, (tick - walkStart) * walkSpeed / 10)` metres. The route is followed from the from node by greedy descent on the cached Dijkstra row of the target node: from node u, step over the edge e that minimises `edgeLen[e] + row[v]` among neighbours v with `row[v] < row[u]`, ties to the lowest edge index. That is a constant cost per hop, no per agent search (hard rule 7). The point is interpolated linearly along the edge.
4. **A separate sample walk cache per grid**, created when the walk graph changes, so a snapshot never evicts a sim row and the sim cache keeps its current behaviour.
5. **Trail cache.** Per cohort slot, derived and never saved or hashed: the last node reached on the route and the distance covered there. A sample resumes from it, so a cohort costs about one hop per snapshot. It is reset when the cohort starts a walk, on load and on any walk graph change. The sample with and without the trail cache is identical.
6. **Snapshot.** New per entry fields `peopleId` (Int32, cohort slot) and `peopleMode` (Uint8: 0 queued, 1 walking, 2 dwelling). `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` goes from 6 to 7. `SNAPSHOT_PEOPLE_CAPACITY` (3 000), `PROFILES.sampleCap` (3 000) and the render figure cap (3 000, 1 500 under the governor) stay.
7. **Sample order per grid:** queued cohorts as today, then walkers, then dwellers, each pass in ascending slot order, until the cap. Curb stage cohorts are not drawn (they have not arrived yet).
8. **Spacing.** One group per 2 m cell, plus a minimum centre gap to every group already drawn in the 8 neighbour cells. The occupancy marks hold the sample index plus one, so the gap test reads 9 cells, constant work.
   * A walker tries its route point, then the points one lane to the left and to the right (perpendicular to the edge, snapped to the dominant grid axis). If none is free it is left out of this sample.
   * A dweller tries the cells of a fixed spiral around its anchor cell (the walk target cell), nearest first, at most `dwellTries` cells.
   * A candidate cell must have floor, no fixture, the same room as the anchor (walkers: as their route point), and the right zone: airside cohorts (`Airside`, `ToGate`) never use a `public` cell, landside cohorts (`ToCheckIn`, `ToSecurity`) never a `secure` cell. This mirrors the queue rule of `serviceNodes.ts`.
9. **Facing.** Walkers face along the edge. Cohorts waiting at a target face the anchor. Airside dwellers face away from the anchor. A dweller on the anchor cell itself faces grid minus y.
10. **Group members.** `packGroup` already keeps member footprints disjoint inside the 2 m cell; its two constants move to data unchanged.
11. **Render interpolation.** The renderer indexes the previous snapshot by `peopleId` with a stamped lookup table of `PROFILES.capacity` entries (no clearing, no allocation per frame), and lerps a matched entry when it moved at most `interpMaxJumpM` on each axis. Walking entries draw the gait frames, picked from game time, profile walk speed and `strideM`, offset by member index and seed. Other modes draw the standing frame.
12. **Migration 26 to 27** writes `walkStart = -1` for every saved slot. Older cohorts are drawn again once they start their next walk.
13. Stress prints a `snapshot` info line (mean ms of `writeSnapshot`, called every 10 ticks outside the tick timing). Not budgeted, not in the baseline.

## Proposed values (owner to confirm)

| Key | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `profiles.json` `walkSample.laneOffsetCells` | 1 | One 2 m cell, the occupancy unit |
| `profiles.json` `walkSample.minGapM` | 1.9 | The packed group width (`cellHalfM` times 2), so full groups never overlap |
| `profiles.json` `walkSample.dwellRingCells` | 3 | Spiral radius, 6 m around the anchor |
| `profiles.json` `walkSample.dwellTries` | 24 | Bounded cost per dweller, about half the 7 by 7 spiral |
| `people.json` `motion.strideM` | 0.7 | Typical adult step length |
| `people.json` `motion.interpMaxJumpM` | 6 | Above a lane shift (2 m) plus a walk step at the fastest speed, below a slot reused across the hall |
| `people.json` `motion.memberMarginM` | 0.05 | Today's `PACK_MARGIN_M`, moved to data |
| `people.json` `motion.cellHalfM` | 0.95 | Today's `CELL_HALF_M`, moved to data |

## Open questions for the owner

* **Q1 Airside dwell place.** Proposed default above: airside cohorts stand on free cells around the security lane they left, with no change to the sim. The alternative is a gameplay rule: walk to the nearest seating with free seats and sit, and start the gate walk from there (changes walk times; seating moves to props in T76).
* **Q2 Seated pose.** No seated figure exists in the atlas or in `people2.py`. Default: everyone is drawn standing; seated drawing waits for art.
* **Q3 Walkers after an edit.** Default: unchanged journey, walkers replan from the curb when due (a jump on screen). The alternative replans from the current route point and changes walk times.

## Requirements

1. The five route arrays exist per slot, are set by every walk, reset to `walkStart = -1` when a cohort is generated, are saved, restored and folded into the hash.
2. `SAVE_VERSION` is 27 with a migration from 26 that sets `walkStart` to -1 for every saved slot.
3. `walkDistance.ts` takes the cache as a parameter internally and exports `distanceRowIn` and `nextEdge`; `walkDistance` keeps its behaviour and results.
4. `src/sim/cohorts/walkSample.ts` exports `cellAt` and `routePoint`, pure and allocation free.
5. The snapshot carries `peopleId` and `peopleMode`; version 7.
6. `writeSample(views, tick)` writes queued, walking and dwelling entries per decisions 7 to 9, without allocation except when a walk graph changed, and without changing the state hash.
7. The trail cache gives the same sample as a fresh computation.
8. Every new number is in `profiles.json` or `people.json` with a strict zod schema and a `note`.
9. The renderer interpolates people by id and animates walkers.
10. The stress run prints the snapshot info line; the medium budget of 4 ms holds and no subsystem regresses by more than 10 percent.

## Acceptance criteria

* Determinism: 2 000 ticks twice from one seed with walking cohorts give identical hashes.
* A save taken while cohorts walk restores to the same hash and, after one more tick on both, the same people sample, then runs on to the same hash.
* A version 26 save migrates and loads with `walkStart` -1 on every slot.
* A walking cohort's sample point lies on its route, gets closer to the target as ticks pass, and matches walk speed within one edge.
* No two entries of a sample share a 2 m cell, and no two non queued entries are closer than `minGapM`.
* Writing a snapshot does not change the state hash.
* In the dev build passengers walk from the curb to check in, to security and to the gates, stand around the security exit while airside, and queue as before. Screenshot with `npm run shot` against `docs/art/people_sheet.png`.
* Full `npm run verify` green, medium under 4 ms, no baseline update.
