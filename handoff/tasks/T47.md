# T47. Stand markings per the sheet

Source: `docs/DESIGN_REVISION_3.md` section 3, `docs/DESIGN_REVISION_4.md` section 2.3 and 2.4, user answers of 2026-11-17 in `handoff/QUESTIONS.md`. Look target: `docs/art/surfaces_stands.png`, followed exactly for nose in stands.

## Goal

Every nose in stand paints the marking set of the sheet, generated from stand size, type, surface and the assigned aircraft types, and every stand shows its connection state on the map.

## Read first

`docs/DESIGN_REVISION_3.md` section 3, `docs/art/surfaces_stands.png`, `src/render/markings/standLayout.ts`, `src/render/markings/standPaint.ts`, `src/data/stand_sizes.json` (markings block), `handoff/PITFALLS.md`.

## Decisions

* Contact stands are drawn as access type 1 until T66. Remote stands keep the bus bay in place of the bridge zone.
* Taxi through markings come with T72, MARS markings with the MVP 2 MARS task.
* Dimensions the docs do not give are measured from the sheet at 7.5 px per meter and stored in the markings block. Sim values (stop bar gap, nose clearance, hydrants, the 12 m service road) stay as they are.
* Connection icons: no taxiway (red) and breaks taxiway clearance (amber), at every zoom.

## Requirements

1. Wing stop outline: the solid red boundary runs down both sides, then angles in at the sheet angle (4 m over the run to the nose line, 15 m before the stand top) and runs straight to the road, closed by a line at the road edge.
2. Double restraint line: the solid line plus a dashed red line inside it, and a dashed red line at both stand edges.
3. Stop ladder: one short yellow bar per type across the centerline with a black box and white type code on both sides.
4. Push limit: a white bar across the centerline with the text PUSH LIMIT.
5. Fuel pits: a filled red square with the text FUEL below it, on stands with hydrants.
6. Emergency equipment zone: a red hatched triangle in the front corner away from the bridge, the text EMERGENCY EQUIPMENT ONLY above it and a fire point symbol.
7. Bridge no parking band: red hatched under the bridge with the text NO PARKING and a fire point. Remote stands draw the bus bay there instead.
8. GSE parking: a white outlined box beside the no parking band with white hatching at its base and the text GSE.
9. GPU spot: a small yellow square with the text GPU beside the nose.
10. Service road: edge lines, center dashes, lane arrows, a zebra crossing near the stand edge and a black STOP box with yellow text on the stand centerline.
11. General aviation: T marks at the wingtips and a stop bar on paved stands, edge markers and tie down rings on gravel, cones and rings on grass and dirt, the stand label in a yellow box (white on grass).
12. Text reads upright with the terminal at the bottom, as on the sheet. Every label fits its box.
13. Connection icons and the box outline on stands without a lead in.
14. At low zoom only the lead in line, the boundary and the stand number show.

## Acceptance

* For every size, no marking zone (emergency triangle, no parking band, GSE box, GPU spot) intersects an assigned type outline.
* The layout is deterministic: the same stand gives the same layout.
* Screenshots of a medium and a large contact stand, a remote stand and the three GA surfaces match the sheet by structure.
