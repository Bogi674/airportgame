# T68. Staff as individuals: role, shift, home building, breaks, sprites, automatic staffing, coverage overlay

Phase 3. Spec written 2026-11-19 on branch `track/t54` after T54, T75 and T67. It replaces T28 (staff sprites) and T33 (automatic staffing and coverage overlay). It runs in parallel with T20 in a separate worktree, so it leaves the arrival journey (arrival stages, immigration, claim, customs journey code in `src/sim/cohorts/cohorts.ts`) alone.

## Goal

Airport staff are individuals. Each has a role, a shift, a home building, a station, a skill and a break. Automatic staffing hires enough staff for every staffed fixture over three shifts plus a relief pool, within a cap the player may set per role. Staff clock in at the staff center, walk to their station on the staff walking rows, relieve the outgoing shift at the station, take a break at the staff center while a relief covers, and clock out at the staff center. A station serves at the speed of the staff present on it, so missing staff lengthen queues, and the passenger satisfaction of T67 carries that into the rating. Staff are drawn with their uniforms, and a staffing coverage overlay shows every station that is short.

Phase exit part owned here: staff change shifts at the staff center, and the rating reacts.

## Facts found in the code

* Staff today is a number: `Business.staffCount()` sums `modules.<key>.staff` of `business.json` over fixture totals plus stands and runways, and `chargeDaily` books `staffCount() * fees.wageStaffPerDay` (130). `tests/business.test.ts` asserts that sum, so its meaning stays.
* The staff center is a T65 `Building` record (kind `staff`, `ground.json` 30 by 20 m, 90 000) on the airfield, outside every terminal grid. No walking graph exists outside the terminal (T66 decision), so travel between a building and a terminal is straight line time.
* T75 gave staff their own distance rows: `walkDistance(wg, from, to, AUD.Staff)` uses `wg.staffCache`. `distanceRowIn`, `nextEdge` and `routePoint` (the people sample) are passenger only.
* Service nodes carry `servers`, `meanS` and a derived `blocked` flag (baggage back pressure, carried over by `rebuildServiceNodes`). `serviceUpdate` divides the service time by `servers`.
* Staffed fixture kinds that are service nodes today: `checkIn`, `bagDrop`, `security`, `passport`, `gateDesk`. `customs` and `eGate` are fixture keys but not service keys yet (T20 may add customs). `securityExit` is not a service node. Screening machines are baggage nodes with stable record ids (`Baggage.nodesOf`).
* The game starts at 06:00 (`START_MINUTE`); a game minute is 50 ticks, a game hour 3 000, a day 72 000. The first daily close is 54 000 ticks after the start.
* The people sample is written by `Cohorts.writeSample` up to `min(PROFILES.sampleCap, SNAPSHOT_PEOPLE_CAPACITY)` (both 3 000). An entry has profile, size, seed, heading, id and mode. `PeopleMotion.walkFrame` indexes walk speed by profile.
* `people.json` holds 30 passenger recipes; `peopleShapes.ts` already draws every staff layer (police cap, beret, pilot cap, helmets, vests, turnout, paramedic, apron, overalls, epaulettes, radio, gloves, wands, toolbox, medbag, extinguisher, cart) through `PersonLookExtras`. The atlas is one row per recipe plus a shadow row; at tier 2 a row is 4 by 103 px.
* Save version is 30. T20 runs in parallel and will likely take 31.

## Decisions

1. **Own store on the cohort substrate pattern.** Staff live in `src/sim/staff/staff.ts`, a struct of arrays of individuals (hard rule 5) with the patterns of `Cohorts`: slots, traits hashed from a saved seed with `mix32` (no rng stream), states with wake ticks, walks recorded as from and to cells for the sample. Staff are not cohort slots: adding staff stages to `Cohorts.Stage` would collide with T20's arrival stages and every stage range check of the passenger journey, and staff are always groups of one. (Rev 4 section 6.4 "Staff use the same substrate", read as the same struct of arrays, traits and walking model.)
2. **Roles in T68** are the ones that have a station in a terminal today (GAME_DESIGN section 10 roles): `agent` (check in agent: check in, bag drop, gate desk), `security` (security officer: security lane, screening machine), `border` (customs and immigration officer: passport control, customs). `STAFF_ROLE_KEYS = ['agent', 'security', 'border']` is a saved index, append only. Ground handlers, marshallers, cleaners, maintenance, retail staff, firefighters, paramedics, police and administrators keep the legacy count until their own task; their sprites ship now (decision 14).
3. **Stations** are derived, never saved: every service node whose fixture key has an entry in `staff.json` `stations`, and every screening machine. A station has a role and a `need` (officers on duty). Stations are rebuilt when the business `serviceVersion` or `terminalVersion`, the baggage `drawVersion` or the grid count changes. A staff member stores its station as grid id, logical cell and fixture kind, never a node id (PITFALLS 25, 26).
4. **Shifts** (GAME_DESIGN section 10: 3 shifts per day): every staff member belongs to one of three shifts with start hours from data. A shift lasts `lengthH`.
5. **Automatic staffing** (T33 R1): a pass hires, per role and per shift, `need` staff for every station plus a relief pool of `ceil(reliefShare * on duty need of the role)`, within the player's cap for the role (default auto, no cap). Staff fill stations in station order, shifts starting from the running one, so a cap leaves the latest stations and later shifts short. Surplus staff are released only while off site. The pass runs when stations change, when a cap changes, and every `passEveryMin`. It is deterministic and a no-op when nothing changed, so a restore may run it again.
6. **Hiring.** A new hire gets the next hire serial; its seed is `mix32(serial)`. A hire for the running shift starts on its station at once, so a newly built desk opens without waiting for a shift change and old saves keep their terminals working. Every other hire waits off site for its shift. There is no hire fee.
7. **Home building.** At clock in a staff member takes the staff center nearest by straight line to the curb door of its station's terminal (ties: lowest building id) as its home; with none its home is the curb door itself (home -1).
8. **Shift change at the staff center** (rev 4 section 5): an incoming member leaves off site so it reaches its station at the shift start: clock in at home (`changeMin`), commute from home to the terminal curb door (straight line at the crew walk speed of `profiles.json`, 1.5 m/s; zero with home -1), walk from the curb door to the station on the staff rows (`AUD.Staff`). On arrival it relieves the outgoing member on that station, who walks to the curb door, commutes home and clocks out (`changeMin`). An outgoing member that is not relieved stays until `overtimeMaxMin` past its shift end, then leaves the station uncovered. A station is never left by a handover before its relief stands on it.
9. **Breaks** (rev 4 sections 5 and 6.4: needs such as breaks): one break per shift, due `afterMin` plus a seeded jitter of 0 to `jitterMin` after the shift start, lasting `lengthMin` at the home staff center. When due, the member calls a relief of its role and shift that is on standby; the relief walks to the station, takes over, and the member goes on break, comes back and relieves the relief, who returns to standby. With no relief within `reliefWaitMin` the member leaves anyway and the station runs short. **With no staff center a member cannot take a break: from the moment it is due its skill counts `tiredSkill` times for the rest of the shift (proposed rule, question 1).**
10. **Skill** (rev 4 section 6.4): a trait from the seed, uniform in `skill.min` to `skill.max`. A station's service speed factor is the skill sum of the members on it (tired members times `tiredSkill`) over its need; 0 when nobody is on it. `serviceUpdate` and `serviceWait` divide by `servers * factor`; a factor of 0 starts no service, like `blocked`. A screening machine with nobody on it screens nothing (closes the T43 backlog entry). Nodes that need no staff keep factor 1.
11. **Rating** reacts through the existing T67 terms: a short station queues longer, queue minutes over patience and missed flights lower member satisfaction, and the daily close feeds the rating. No new rating part.
12. **Wages** (T33 R3): each staff member costs its role's `wagePerDay`, booked in `chargeDaily` with the wages. `staffCount()` keeps its meaning (positions estimated from fixtures, stands and runways) for the roles that are not individuals yet; the daily wage is `round((staffCount() - stationPositions()) * wageStaffPerDay) + staffWages`, where `stationPositions()` is the `modules.<key>.staff` sum of the station fixture keys. The finance line shows hired staff plus that legacy remainder.
13. **Player control** (GAME_DESIGN "manual or auto"): command `setStaffCap { role, cap }`, cap -1 for auto. A manual roster per person is out of scope.
14. **Sprites** (T28, ART section 6.3): `people.json` gains a `staff` list of 16 recipes from the sheet's Airport staff row, baked into the same atlas after the passenger rows (atlas grows from 31 to 47 rows, about 8.0 MB at tier 2, inside the 8 MB per tier of ART section 2). `staff.json` maps each role to its sprite ids (security has two variants). Only roles of decision 2 are drawn in T68.
15. **People sample.** Staff append to the same snapshot people arrays after the passengers: profile byte `STAFF_PROFILE_BASE + role` (32 + role), size 1, seed the staff seed, id `STAFF_ID_BASE + slot` (1 << 30, never a cohort slot), mode Walking or Dwelling. The passenger sample cap drops to make room (`profiles.json` `sampleCap`). On duty staff stand behind their fixture, `operator.backM` from the anchor centre away from the queue, spread `operator.spacingM` apart when a station needs more than one; walkers follow their staff route. Staff off site, commuting, clocking in or on break are not drawn (no walking graph outside the terminal). Snapshot layout is unchanged, so `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 7.
16. **Coverage overlay** (T33 R4, UI_DESIGN Overlays): overlay kind `staffing`. `OverlayData` gains `stationCell`, `stationNeed`, `stationPresent`; each station cell is filled on the ok, warn, bad ramp by its shortfall in percent (0 full, 100 nobody), legend unit "% short", fixed scale 0 to 100. Derived, never saved or hashed.
17. **Staff sheet** (UI_DESIGN left rail 9 and side sheets): a sheet per role with hired, on duty, away (commuting, at the staff center, on break), needed on duty, cap (Auto or a number with a stepper) and wage per day. Opened from the Staff category of the build menu.
18. **Tick order.** `staff.update(tick)` runs in subsystem 3 before `business.update`, so the service factors and the screening flags of a tick always follow the staff state of that tick and a restore needs no extra fix up. The station check runs every tick (version compare only); states advance every `stepMin`. No allocation outside station rebuilds.
19. **Save version 32** (31 if T20 does not land first; the orchestrator renumbers at merge). `SaveData.staff`: `hi`, hire `serial`, `caps`, and the packed slot arrays. Migration: a version bump; older saves have no staff and the first pass hires the running shift on its stations (decision 6).
20. **Hash order**: after `turns`: `hi`, `serial`, caps, then per used slot every saved array in declaration order.
21. **Out of scope, new roadmap row at close**: apron crews (ground handlers, marshallers, baggage handlers) on turnarounds, cleaners, staffed security exits, staff screening queues for staff routes, morale, fatigue beyond the break, sick leave, training and the yearly wage index.

## Questions for the owner

1. No staff center: proposed default is that staff still work, clock in and out at the curb door and skip their break, working at `tiredSkill` 0.8 once it is due. Alternative: no staffing at all without a staff center (every staffed desk closes), which would stop every existing save. Proceed with the default unless the owner says otherwise.
2. Apron crews, cleaners and staffed security exits as a new roadmap row after T68 (decision 21). Proceed with the default.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Data validates; a station with an unknown role, a need of 0, a skill min above max, or a role sprite id outside `people.json` staff fails.
2. Determinism: two sims from one seed with the test terminal and a staff center, 2 000 ticks, identical `stateHash`; and 26 000 ticks across the 14:00 shift change, identical.
3. Save during the 14:00 handover and during a break, restore into a fresh sim, run 2 000 more ticks: same hash as the uninterrupted sim. A version 30 save migrates to 32 and its first update hires the running shift on its stations.
4. Exit test: with a staff center, at 14:00 every station of the test terminal is relieved by shift 1 staff who clocked in at the staff center (its clock in counter grows by the station need sum), shift 0 staff clock out there, and no station has zero staff present at any step from 13:30 to 14:30.
5. Rating reacts: two sims, one with `setStaffCap { role: security, cap: 0 }`; at the first daily close the capped sim has no passenger through security, a lower `Business.satisfaction` and a lower `rating`.
6. A cap of 1 security officer on a lane that needs 2 halves its service speed (factor 0.5 times skill) and its queue grows against the uncapped run.
7. A screening machine with no officer screens nothing; with one it screens as before.
8. Breaks: with a staff center a member leaves for its break only after a relief stands on its station; without one the member stays and its skill counts 0.8 from its break time.
9. Wages: the day's wages equal the legacy remainder plus the sum of hired role wages.
10. The people sample shows staff behind their desks and walking; screenshot of the test terminal compared with `docs/art/people_sheet.png`; no staff figure shares a spot with a passenger figure.
11. The staffing overlay shows full stations ok, a half staffed lane warn and an empty desk bad; legend reads "% short".
12. The Staff sheet lists the three roles with live counts and the cap stepper sends `setStaffCap`.
13. Full `npm run verify` green, medium and people20k under 4 ms, no regression, baseline untouched.
