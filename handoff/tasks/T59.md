# T59 Stand classes, placement, lead in snapping, connection states

## Goal

Stands become single prefabs in four sizes with a nose in or pass through layout, set to take GA, commercial or cargo flights in any mix. The player places one stand at a time with rotation, the lead in snaps to a taxiway centerline, and the ghost always shows the box and why it is red.

## Read first

* `docs/DESIGN_REVISION_4.md` section 2, with the user answers of 2026-11-17 in `handoff/QUESTIONS.md`, which replace D1 and D2.
* `handoff/reference/benchmark-stands.md`.
* `src/shared/standFork.ts`, `src/shared/standGeom.ts`, `src/shared/flightBlock.ts`, `src/sim/ops/traffic.ts` (stand choice), `src/ui/tools/controller.ts` (stand tool).

## Decisions

1. Four sizes stay: small, medium, large, extra large. No GA class.
2. `Stand.accepts` is a bit set: GA 1, commercial 2, cargo 4. Never empty. It replaces `Stand.usage`.
3. A flight of the GA airline needs the GA bit, every other flight the commercial bit. Cargo is stored only until cargo flights exist.
4. Automatic choice: the aircraft's own size first, then the next larger, never smaller. Manual assignment may use any larger stand.
5. Layout `noseIn` (default) or `passThrough`. Pass through has no push back, enters from the tail side and leaves forward on a centerline ahead of the nose. It never gets a jet bridge.
6. Single stands: the stand tool has no count. The GA block tool keeps its rows of small stands that accept GA only.
7. R rotates 15 degrees, Shift with R rotates 1 degree. The lead in snaps within 60 m of the box tail edge (D3).
8. Jet bridges are T66 and need a second terminal floor.

## Requirements

1. Save version 16 migrates `usage`: `ga` to 1, `commercial` to 2, `any` or missing to 3 for small remote stands and 2 otherwise.
2. `setStandProps` takes `accepts`; a zero value is refused.
3. The refusal `standUsage` names the missing flight category.
4. Stand choice scores by size class distance, own class first.
5. The stand inspector shows three toggles for GA, commercial and cargo.
6. The stand tool places one stand, rotates with R, faces the nearest centerline within reach until the player rotates, and slides along its axis so its tail edge sits at the size's fork radius from the centerline.
7. The stand tool ghost is red with `stand.noLeadIn` when the tail edge is more than the reach from the lead in centerline. The sim keeps accepting such a stand, because an unconnected stand is a valid state (section 2.3, a taxiway demolished later) and many tests build stands on bare land.
8. The ghost always draws the stand box, red with the first failed check named in the context bar.
9. The stand inspector lists the connection states: taxiway connection and taxiway clearance.
10. Pass through stands: layout picker in the stand tool, a lead out from the nose to a centerline ahead, routing out forward with no push back.
11. All new numbers live in `src/data/stand_sizes.json` with a zod schema.

## Acceptance

* An old save loads with the accepts bits of requirement 1 and plays as before.
* A GA flight never takes a stand without the GA bit, a commercial flight never one without the commercial bit.
* A code C aircraft takes a free medium stand before a free large one, and a large one when no medium is free.
* A single stand placed near a taxiway snaps, faces it and turns green; far from any taxiway it shows its box in red with the reason.
* Determinism test: 2 000 ticks twice from one seed give identical hashes.
